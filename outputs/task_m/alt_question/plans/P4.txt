Bidirectional Cis-Trans Count-Likelihood PMI Retrieval with Within-Cluster Deviance Weighting

Rather than compressing cells into a global latent vector, this plan scores cross-modal pairing directly in count space via bidirectional Pointwise Mutual Information. A forward Cis-Attention plus Trans-LSI Negative-Binomial decoder evaluates unsmoothed query RNA counts from candidate ATAC profiles, while a reverse dropout-aware encoder scores candidate ATAC accessibility against a marginal depth baseline. Inner-split excess-deviance gene weights prioritize within-cluster regulatory variation across nested 5-fold cross-validation. Benchmarking against all required floors, linear alignment, and split-half ceilings establishes whether retrieval achieves single-cell, sub-cluster, or cluster-only resolution.

Rationale: Simplex-constrained Negative-Binomial and depth-calibrated Bernoulli likelihood ratios separate true repression from sampling dropout (D1) while cancelling depth confounding (D5) and candidate hubness. Inner-split excess-deviance gene weights amplify within-cluster cis-regulatory variation beyond coarse identity (D6), and unsupervised inverse-density weights protect rare states without WNN labels (D2, D4).
