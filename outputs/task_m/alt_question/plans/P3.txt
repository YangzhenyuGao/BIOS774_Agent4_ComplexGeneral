Cis-Regulatory MultiVI Multi-Scale Ensemble with Precision-Weighted Posterior Retrieval

We train a nested 5-fold ensemble of MultiVI variational autoencoders coupling raw RNA UMI counts with cis-regulatory ATAC peaks within 150 kb of in-scope genes. Observed library depths enter solely into count decoders while unsupervised inverse-density sample weights preserve rare cell states without forbidden WNN annotations. Unseen RNA queries independently rank candidate ATAC profiles via precision-weighted Bhattacharyya similarity between unimodal posteriors, attenuating high-dropout axes. Paired comparisons against depth, gene-activity, cluster, linear CCA, and split-half ceiling rulers test whether retrieval achieves single-cell, sub-cluster, or only cell-type resolution.

Rationale: Negative Binomial and Bernoulli decoders separate biological state from library-size confounding (D5) while modeling extreme count sparsity (D1). Precision-weighted posterior matching suppresses uncertain dropout dimensions during retrieval, and unsupervised inverse-density weighting plus 5-fold pooling protect rare populations like 15-cell Plasma states (D2) against coarse cluster baselines (D6).
