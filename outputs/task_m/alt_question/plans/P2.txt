Hard-Negative Dual-View Contrastive Bi-Encoder with Depth Deconfounding

We train paired residual neural encoders with symmetric InfoNCE contrastive loss to retrieve an unseen cell's unsmoothed ATAC profile from its RNA counts alone. The ATAC encoder combines sparse TF-IDF peaks and promoter-and-distal gene activity while penalizing sequencing-depth shortcuts. Training uses binomial thinning, unsupervised inverse-cluster weighting, and an intra-cluster hard-negative batch curriculum fitted strictly on inner-training cells. Benchmarking on held-out queries (and an expanded 4,946-cell distractor pool) against required floors, linear CCA, and split-half ceilings determines whether retrieval exceeds cluster resolution.

Rationale: Linear SVD discards non-linear enhancer combinations and remains vulnerable to sequencing-depth shortcuts (D5). Pairing sparse peaks with cis-linked gene activity under binomial thinning counteracts extreme sparsity (D1). Unsupervised inverse-cluster weights and intra-cluster hard negatives address rare populations (D2) and sub-cluster retrieval beyond coarse centroids (D6).
